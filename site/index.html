the best person I know

Who is Grace?

Grace is the best person I know. She doesn’t always put great value in herself, but she makes the world brighter for me every day. — Garrett

She is silly when you least expect it and serious about the things that matter. She has carried chronic pain with a strength most people never see, and she still spends her energy worrying about everyone else first. Dizzy days, brain fog, and one too many of my dad jokes have never once dimmed how much she cares.

You think of others before yourself, every time. The world is gentler because you are in it.

You cannot stand to see anyone mistreated, and you will say so. That moral compass never wavers.

Taylor Swift and Stray Kids on repeat, a dance in the kitchen, and a voice I would follow anywhere.

You want to please God and do His will, and you live it out quietly, in a thousand small kindnesses.

A good nap, a bean burrito, froyo on a warm day, and Pride and Prejudice for the hundredth time.
